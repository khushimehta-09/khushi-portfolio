SecureAuth — Distributor Management Portal

Complete Django authentication and distributor management portal built with dual roles, distributor approval workflows, email OTPs, login lockouts, and audit trails.

Problem Solved:

B2B distributor operations require strict identity verification, administrative approval gating before portal access, brute-force defense against unauthorized logins, and transparent audit logging for account updates.

Key Implemented Features:

  • Custom Django User Architecture: Distinct Admin and Distributor models with granular permissions.
  • Distributor Approval Lifecycle: Automatic Pending status upon sign-up; Admin approves, rejects, blocks, or provisions users.
  • Cryptographic 6-Digit Email OTP: Timed 5-minute expiration window for verified password reset requests.
  • Brute-Force Lockout Defense: Automatic 10-minute access lockout triggered after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.
  • Audit Trail & Activity Tracking: Comprehensive capture of IP address, browser user-agent, and timestamp for all sign-ins.
  • Distributor Support Ticket Desk: Bi-directional ticket submission and admin response workflow.
